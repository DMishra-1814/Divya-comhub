Kaha Kaha Zarurat Padti Hai Product Management Ki?

Har company jo product banati hai, waha PM ki zarurat hai!

💻

Tech Companies

Google, Amazon, Flipkart, Microsoft me app aur website manage karne ke liye.

📱

Mobile Apps

Zomato, Swiggy, Instagram, Paytm jaise apps me naye features lane ke liye.

🛍️

E-Commerce

Product listing, payment, delivery track karne wale system ke liye.

🏦

Fintech & Banking

PhonePe, CRED, HDFC Bank ke apps ko safe aur easy banane ke liye.

🧴

FMCG

HUL, P&G, Nestle me naya product jaise shampoo ya biscuit launch karne ke liye.

🏥

Healthcare

1mg, Practo jaise health apps aur medicines delivery ke liye.

🎓

EdTech

Byju's, Unacademy, PhysicsWallah me courses aur learning experience ke liye.

🚗

Automobile

Tesla, Ola Electric me nayi gadi ke features plan karne ke liye.

Simple Line Me Samjho:

Jaha Customer hai + Jaha Product hai = Waha Product Manager ki zarurat hai. Aaj ke time me har badi company me 10-50 Product Managers hote hi hain.
