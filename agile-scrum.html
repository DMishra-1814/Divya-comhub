⚡ Agile & Scrum Kya Hai?

PM ki sabse important working style!

🤔 Agile Kya Hai?

Agile ek sochne ka tarika hai. Bada kaam ek baar me karne ki jagah chhote-chhote tukdo me karo, jaldi feedback lo.

Agile ke 4 Rules:
1. Logo pe focus, process pe nahi
2. Kaam karne wala product, documents se better
3. Customer se baat karo
4. Change ko welcome karo

🔄 Scrum Kya Hai?

Scrum Agile ko karne ka framework hai. Jaise cricket ka format T20 hai, waise hi Agile ka format Scrum hai.

📅 Scrum Kaise Chalta Hai?

Product Backlog → Sprint Planning → 2 Week Sprint → Daily Standup → Review
3 Main Roles:
👩‍💼 Product Owner (Tu): Kya banana hai decide karti hai
🧑‍🏫 Scrum Master: Team ko help karta hai
👨‍💻 Dev Team: Kaam banata hai

⚔️ Waterfall vs Agile

❌ Waterfall
6 mahine plan
Fir 6 mahine banana
End me pata chala user ko pasand nahi
✅ Agile/Scrum
2 week me chhota feature
User ko dikhao
Feedback se sudharo
