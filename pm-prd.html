📄 PRD - Zomato Late Delivery Fixv1.0
1. Problem
User ko khana late aane se gussa aata hai, 40% complaint isi ki hai. Order cancel kar dete hai.

2. Goal
Late delivery complaint ko 30% kam karna hai 2 mahine me.

3. User Story
"As a user, I want delivery boy ki live location + exact time, taki mujhe wait na karna pade"

4. Solution
- Live Map + 5 min ka timer
- Agar 10 min late toh 50 Rs coupon auto

5. Success Metric
Complaint < 20%, Coupon use > 60%

Yaad Karne Ka Trick

PRD = P Problem + R Requirement + D Delivery Plan

"PRD dev team ke liye love letter hai, jitna clear likhogi utna jaldi feature banega"
