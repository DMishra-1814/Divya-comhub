What is Product Management?

Product Management is the art of creating the right product for the right people at the right time.

Simple Definition

Product Management ek aisa process hai jisme ek product ko idea se leke launch tak aur uske baad bhi manage kiya jata hai. Product Manager bridge hota hai Customer, Business aur Technology team ke beech me.

Product Management Ki Jarurat Kaha Padti Hai?

🚀 IT & Tech Companies

Google, Amazon, Microsoft, Flipkart jaise bade tech products banane ke liye.

🛒 E-Commerce

Website aur App pe user ka experience behtar karne ke liye.

📱 Startups & Apps

Zomato, Swiggy, Paytm jaise naye ideas ko product me badalne ke liye.

🏭 FMCG Companies

HUL, P&G jaise companies ko naye soap, shampoo launch karne ke liye.

Product Manager Kya Karta Hai? (Main Roles)

1. Customer ko Samjhna: Customer ki problem kya hai?

2. Roadmap Banana: Product me kya feature aayega, kab aayega?

3. Team Management: Designers aur Developers ke saath kaam karna.

4. Launch & Improve: Product ko launch karna aur feedback se improve karna.
