🎯 PM Interview Q&A

20 Sawal = Job Pakka!

🌟 Har answer ko STAR se bolo - Situation, Task, Action, Result 🌟

📝 Basic Wale (1-7)

1 Product Manager kya karta hai?
PM team ka captain hai. User ki problem samjhta hai, solution sochta hai, aur dev team se banwata hai. Mini-CEO bhi bolte hai.
2 Good Product Manager me kya quality honi chahiye?
1. User se pyaar (Empathy) 2. Data se decision 3. Communication clear 4. Priority set karna aata ho
3 Roadmap kya hai?
Product ka GPS hai. Agle 6-12 mahine me kya banega, kab banega, kyu banega - iska plan.
4 MVP kya hai?
Sabse chhota product jo kaam kare. Jaise car se pehle skateboard banao, feedback lo, fir car banao.

🚀 Advance Wale (8-15)

8 Agar Zomato ka order late ho raha hai toh kaise fix karogi?
Pehle data dekhti - kaha late ho raha hai? Restaurant ya Rider? Fir user se baat karti. Solution: Sahi delivery time pehle dikhana + Restaurant ko auto-alert. Metric: Late % 20% se 5% karna.
9 Ek feature ka priority kaise decide karogi?
RICE Model se - Reach (kitne log), Impact (kitna fayda), Confidence (kitna sure), Effort (kitna time). Score zyada = pehle banao.
10 WhatsApp me ek naya feature kya add karogi?
User: Small business wale. Problem: Payment yaad dilana padta hai. Feature: Auto Payment Reminder. Kyu: User ka time bachega, WhatsApp ka use badhega.
11 North Star Metric kya hai?
Ek number jo bataye product sahi ja raha hai. WhatsApp ka Daily Message, Amazon ka Monthly Purchase. Meri site ka Monthly Form Fill.
12 Agar dev team bole ye feature nahi ban sakta?
Kyu nahi ban sakta samjhti. Fir MVP sochti - chhota version ban sakta hai kya? Ya dusra easy solution hai kya? Ladti nahi, saath me solution nikalti.

💥 Last Wale (16-20)

16 Tum PM kyu banna chahti ho?
Mujhe problem solve karna pasand hai. Apni Divya ComHub site pe maine dekha kaise user ko form bharne me help chahiye, toh maine WhatsApp help add kiya aur 10% se 35% ho gaya. Yehi kaam bade level pe karna hai.
20 Hum tumhe kyu hire kare?
Kyunki mai user ki bhasha samajhti hu (Hindi + Tech), khud website bana ke 12 pages likhe hai, aur fail fast - learn fast me vishwas karti hu. Mujhe mauka do, mai metric badha ke dikhaungi.
