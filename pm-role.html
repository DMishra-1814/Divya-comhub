👩‍💼 PM Ka Role Kya Hai?

Mini-CEO of Product 👑

🎯 Ek Line Me: PM ka kaam hai Sahi Product, Sahi Logo Ke Liye, Sahi Time Pe Banana

🔄 5 Main Roles

👂
1. User Ka Dost
User se baat karo, uski problem samjho. Roz 2 user se call karo. Jaise doctor patient se puchta hai.
🧠
2. Sochne Wala (Strategist)
Product kaha jayega 1 saal me? Kaunsa feature pehle? Roadmap banao.
👷
3. Team Ka Captain
Designer ko bolo kya design karna hai, Developer ko bolo kya code karna hai. Sabko saath leke chalo.
📊
4. Data Wala
Number dekho - Kitne log aaye? Kitno ne form bhara? Data se decide karo, guess se nahi.
💰
5. Business Wala
Paise kaise ayenge? Ads se? Subscription se? Company ko profit kaise hoga socho.

⏰ Ek Din Ka Routine

9 AM ☕ Standup 10 AM 👂 User Call 11 AM 🎨 Figma Review 1 PM 📋 Jira Check 3 PM 📊 Data Dekho 5 PM 🗺️ Roadmap Plan

Yehi hota hai PM ka din - Meeting, User, Data, Team!
💡 Yaad Rakho: PM code nahi likhta, design nahi banata, par sabko sahi direction deta hai. Jaise cricket me Captain!
