Role of a Product Manager

CEO of the Product - Bina Degree Wala CEO!

Product Manager karta kya hai?

Product Manager ko Product ka Mini-CEO bola jata hai. Uska kaam hai sochna, plan karna, aur banwana.

🎯

1. Customer Ki Problem Samjhna

Customer interview karna, survey karna, data dekhna ki user ko actual me kya dikkat hai.

📝

2. PRD Banana (Plan Likna)

Developers aur Designers ke liye ek document banana ki product me kya-kya feature hoga.

👥

3. Team Ko Manage Karna

Design, Tech, Marketing sabko ek saath leke chalna.

📊

4. Product Launch & Growth

Product ko launch karna aur uske numbers (users, profit) track karna.

Din bhar kya karta hai PM?

Subah: Team meeting (Scrum)

Dopahar: Customer calls + Data Analysis

Shaam: Roadmap planning + CEO ko update
