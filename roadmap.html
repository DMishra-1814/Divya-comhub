🗺️ Product Roadmap

Product ka GPS - Bina iske team rasta bhool jayegi!

Roadmap ek strategic plan hai jo batata hai product ko kaha le jana hai aur kaise.

💡 Kyu Jaruri Hai?

✦ Team ko clear vision milta hai
✦ Stakeholders ko timeline pata chalti hai
✦ Priority set karna easy hota hai

📚 Roadmap Ke 3 Types

🎯 Feature Roadmap: Kaunsa feature kab ayega
📅 Timeline Roadmap: Date-wise full plan
🚀 Goal Roadmap: User problem solve pe focus

🛠️ Kaise Banate Hai? (4 Steps)

1
Vision Likho
1 saal baad product kaha dekhna chahti ho?
2
User Problem Samjho
User ki asli takleef kya hai?
3
Priority - RICE Model
Reach, Impact, Confidence, Effort
4
Timeline Banao
Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4 me divide karo
