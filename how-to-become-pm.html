How to Become a Product Manager in 2026? - Complete Roadmap

Many students want to become PM after college. Here is a simple 5-step roadmap for beginners.

Step 1: Learn the Basics

Understand what is Product Management, Agile, Scrum, and Product Lifecycle. Read free blogs and watch YouTube videos.

Step 2: Build Required Skills

Focus on Communication, Data Analysis (Excel/SQL), Basic UI/UX with Figma, and Problem Solving.

Step 3: Do Projects

Create case studies. Pick any app like Swiggy or Instagram and write how you would improve it. Add to your portfolio.

Step 4: Learn Tools

Learn Jira for task management, Figma for design, Notion for docs, and Google Analytics for data.

Step 5: Apply for APM / Internships

Start as Associate Product Manager (APM) or Product Intern. Many companies hire freshers for this role.

Is MBA Required?

No, MBA is not compulsory. Skills and case studies are more important than degree for PM role.
