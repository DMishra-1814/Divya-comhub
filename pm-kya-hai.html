👨‍💼 PM Kya Hota Hai?

Basti | Simple Me Samjho

🤔 Simple Definition

Product Manager matlab Product ka Maa-Baap - Jaise ghar me mummy sab sambhalti hai, waise PM product sambhalta hai. User ko kya chahiye, dev ko kya banana hai, business ko paise kaise ayenge - sab PM sochta hai.

🎬 Filmy Example

Socho Film banti hai - Director = PM
🎥 Hero (Developer) ko batayega kya karna hai
📝 Story (User Problem) samjhega
💰 Producer (Business) ko profit dilayega

🧠 3 Kaam PM Ke

1️⃣ User Samjho: Logon ko dikkat kya hai?
2️⃣ Solution Banao: Uska hal kya hai?
3️⃣ Team Se Banwao: Design, Dev, QA sabko saath leke chalo
