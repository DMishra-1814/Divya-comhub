📊 PM Metrics Kya Hai?

Jo measure nahi karoge, wo improve nahi karoge!

Metrics matlab Product ka Report Card - Product chal raha hai ya fail ho raha hai kaise pata chalega?

🌟 North Star Metric - Sabse Jaruri

North Star = Ek aisa number jo bataye product sahi direction me hai

WhatsApp ka: Daily Messages Sent
Amazon ka: Monthly Purchases
Teri Divya ComHub ka: Monthly Forms Filled ⭐

📈 4 Main Metrics (AARRR)

DAU/WAUKitne log roz aate hai?
RetentionWapas kitne aaye?
ConversionKitno ne form bhara?
ChurnKitne log chhod gaye?

🎯 Example Se Samjho

Teri Website ka Example:
👥 100 log aaye site pe
👀 60 ne page dekha (60% Engagement)
📝 20 ne form pe click kiya (33% Conversion)
✅ 10 ne form complete kiya (50% Success)

Ab samajh aaya kaha problem hai? Click se Complete tak!
