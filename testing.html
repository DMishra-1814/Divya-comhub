13. 🧪 A/B Testing

A/B Testing kya hai?
50% users ko Design A dikhao, 50% ko Design B.
Jo zyada click kare, wo winner.
Example - ComHub ke liye:
A - Button ka color Yellow
B - Button ka color Pink

100 users me se 70 ne Yellow pe click kiya = Yellow winner.
PM ko kya karna hai?
1. Hypothesis banao
2. 50-50 traffic baanto
3. 7 din chalao
4. Data dekho, winner choose karo
