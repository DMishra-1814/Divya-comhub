🔄 Product Life Cycle

Idea se leke End tak Product ka Safar

💡 1. Ideation

User ki problem ko samjho. Market research karo. Kya banane ki zarurat hai, ye decide hota hai.

📋 2. Planning & Design

Features list banao (PRD), Wireframes aur Figma design banao. Timeline set karo.

👨‍💻 3. Development

Engineers product ko code karte hain. PM daily standups me progress check karta hai.

🚀 4. Launch

Product ko market me launch karo. Marketing team ke saath milke users ko batao.

📈 5. Growth & Feedback

Data dekho, user feedback lo, aur product ko improve karte raho. Ye kabhi khatam nahi hota.
