🤔 PM Kya Hai?

Product Manager = Product ka Maa-Baap. User ka problem samjho, solution banao, team se banwao. PM coding nahi karta, sabko manage karta hai.
2

⚔️ PM vs Project Manager

PM sochta hai - KYA banana hai? Project Manager sochta hai - KAB tak banana hai? PM = What, Project Manager = When.
3

🔄 Product Lifecycle

Idea → Research → Design → Build → Launch → Improve. ComHub ab Launch pe hai, Improve karte rahenge.
4

👤 User Persona

Ex: Divya, 21, BCA 3rd Year, Basti. Problem: PM samajh nahi aata. Goal: Job chahiye. Iske liye site banayi.
5

📝 User Story

As a BCA student, I want PM easy Hindi me padhna, so that interview crack kar saku. Simple format.
6

📄 PRD Kya Hai?

PRD = Product ka blueprint. Kya banana hai, kyu banana hai, kaise pata chalega ban gaya - sab likha hota hai.
7

✏️ Wireframe

Pen-paper wala kacha design. Pehle wireframe, fir Figma me rang-roop. Paise bachate hai.
8

🚀 MVP

Minimum Viable Product. Chhota version jaldi launch karo. ComHub ka MVP = ye 30 pages wala site.
9

🏃 Agile & Scrum

2 hafte ka Sprint. Kaam ko tukdo me baanto. Roz 15 min standup. Fail fast, learn fast.
10

📌 JIRA

Ticket system. To-Do → In Progress → Done. PM ticket banata hai, Dev code karta hai.
11

🔍 Case Study

Kisi app ka teardown. Ex: Zomato me 30 min kyu likha? Search upar kyu? Socho.
12

⭕ CIRCLES Method

Comprehend, Identify, Report, Cut, List, Evaluate, Summarize. Interview me product design ka jawaab isi se do.
13

🧪 A/B Testing

50% ko A dikhao, 50% ko B. Jo zyada click kare wo winner. Ex: Yellow button vs Pink button.
14

💡 Design Thinking

Empathize, Define, Ideate, Prototype, Test. User se pyaar karo, solution apne aap ayega.
15

⚔️ Competitor Analysis

Dusre kya kar rahe dekho, unse better karo. ComHub vs Youtube PM videos - Hum easy Hindi me hai.
16

🎤 User Interview

5 sawal pucho: Ab kaise karte ho? Sabse badi dikkat? Kab hua? Paise doge iske liye? Mera wala loge?
17

📊 KPI

Number jo success bataye. DAU, Time on site. ComHub ka North Star = Weekly Active Learners.
18

📢 GTM - Go To Market

Kaha launch? College WhatsApp group, Insta. Kya bologe? Basti ke liye free me PM sikho.
19

🏢 Business Model

Customer kaun? Problem kya? Solution kya? Paise kaise ayenge? ComHub abhi free hai.
20

💰 Monetization

Ads, Freemium, Subscription. Pehle 1000 user banao, fir ₹49 ka eBook becho.
21

🤝 Stakeholder

Engineer, Designer, CEO sabko ek line pe lana. PM ka sabse mushkil kaam yahi hai.
22

🔢 Estimation / Guesstimate

Ex: 1 din me Swiggy pe kitne orders? Logic: Population x Phone % x Order % = Answer. Formula yaad rakho.
23

🔌 API

Frontend aur Backend ka bridge. Button dabaya → API call gaya → Data aaya → Screen pe dikha. Bas.
24

💻 Tech Stack for PM

PM ko code nahi, tech samajhna hai. HTML, CSS, API, Database, Cloud - itna kaafi hai interview ke liye.
25

📈 Product Metrics - AARRR

Acquisition, Activation, Retention, Revenue, Referral. User aaya, ruka, paise diye, dosto ko bataya.
26

⚖️ Prioritization - RICE

Reach, Impact, Confidence, Effort. Score = (R*I*C)/E. Jiska score zyada, wo pehle banao.
27

🗺️ Roadmap

Now (is mahina), Next (2 mahine), Later (6 mahine). ComHub ka Now = 30 pages complete karna.
28

🎙️ PM Interview Questions

Top Q: Fav product? Improve kaise karoge? Estimation? Behavioral: Team me ladai hui to kya kiya?
29
