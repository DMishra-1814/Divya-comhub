🔍 Product Teardown

Kisi Bhi App Ko PM Ki Tarah Cheer Phaad

4 Step Formula

Step 1: Problem Kya Hai?WHY
Ye app kiski kaunsi problem solve kar raha hai?
Ex: Swiggy = Ghar baithe khana, time bachao
Step 2: Flow Check KaroHOW
App kholo aur 1 kaam karo. Kaha kaha click kiya? Kitna time laga?
Ex: Swiggy pe Order karne me 5 click lagte hai, 2 min
Step 3: Good vs BadREVIEW
✅ Good: Fast delivery, Live tracking, Easy payment
❌ Bad: Delivery charge high, Coupon code kaam nahi karta, Support slow
Step 4: Tera Solution Kya Hai?PM MIND
Agar tu PM hoti toh kya sudharti? Number ke sath bolo.
Ex: "Coupon auto-apply kar deti toh 20% log zyada order karte"
⭐ LIVE EXAMPLE - WhatsApp Teardown by Divya:

Problem: Logon ko fast baat karni hai
Good: Simple hai, sab use karte hai, free hai
Bad: File 2GB se zyada nahi jaati, Laptop pe slow hai
Mera Idea: "WhatsApp me 'Schedule Message' feature add karo - Birthday wish raat 12 baje auto chala jayega. Isse 30% active users badhenge"
